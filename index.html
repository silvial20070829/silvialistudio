About

I'm Silvia, and I enjoy exploring both photography and graphic design. I'm especially drawn to color, light, composition, and the small visual details that can completely change how something feels. I'm always experimenting with new ways to turn things I notice in everyday life into something visual.

This portfolio is a collection of work I've made over the past few years, from photography projects to graphic design experiments. A lot of it comes from simply noticing something interesting and wanting to explore it further. I'm still learning and experimenting, and I'm excited to keep developing these interests through new projects and collaborations in college.

Thanks for taking a look at my work! Feel free to reach out if you'd like to connect or talk more about any of my projects.
